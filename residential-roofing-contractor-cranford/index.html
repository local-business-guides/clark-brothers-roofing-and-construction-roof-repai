What a home roofing project can involve

The service may include looking at the roof’s condition, discussing your priorities, and identifying an appropriate scope of work. The exact work depends on what is needed for your property. Before agreeing to a project, ask what is included and how the work will be organized.

Key facts to understand:
- Coverage: Roofing work is the focus. Siding, gutters, and windows are also among the company’s construction services.
- Timing: The schedule depends on the project scope, materials, and planning. Ask what steps come first and when the contractor expects each stage to happen.
- Cost factors: Roof size and shape, selected materials, access, and the amount of work required can affect the estimate. Ask for the scope and cost factors to be explained; no single figure applies to every home.
- Comparing providers: Compare the proposed work, materials, preparation, cleanup, schedule, and how questions will be handled. Make sure you understand what is included before choosing.
- Terms you may hear: *Roof deck* means the surface beneath the roofing material. *Flashing* is material used around roof joints or openings to help manage water. Ask the contractor to explain any term you’re unsure about.
