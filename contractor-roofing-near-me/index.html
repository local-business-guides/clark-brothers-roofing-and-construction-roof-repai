Project details, timing, and cost factors

The work may be limited to a particular roofing concern or involve a broader plan. The contractor should discuss the roof and clarify the tasks being proposed. Confirm the agreed scope so you know what the project covers and what would require a separate conversation.

Key facts to review:
- Work covered: Ask which roof areas and tasks are included. Mention any interest in siding, gutters, or windows so their connection to the project can be discussed.
- Typical timing: The sequence and schedule vary with the scope, materials, access, and conditions at the property. Ask for a project-specific expectation rather than assuming a set duration.
- General cost influences: Roof dimensions and design, material selections, the amount of work, and related construction needs can all affect cost. Request a breakdown that explains how the proposed work shapes the estimate; no single general figure can describe every roof.

Some common terms are worth clarifying. Assessment is a review of the roof and its needs. Scope is the agreed list of work. Estimate is an expected cost based on the details available, while a proposal describes the suggested work and its cost for your consideration.
