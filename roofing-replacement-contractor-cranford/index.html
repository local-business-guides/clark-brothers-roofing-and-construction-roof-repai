Scope, timing and cost factors to understand

A replacement project is easier to evaluate when the scope is spelled out. Ask what the work includes, what is excluded, and how the contractor will communicate if conditions call for a change. Timing can depend on the size and layout of the roof, its condition, material choices, weather, and scheduling. Ask for an estimated start and duration rather than assuming every project follows the same timetable.

Cost is shaped by the project details, not just the roof’s visible area. Existing conditions, the amount of work, selected materials, and any related exterior work can affect the overall plan. Compare written proposals by checking that they cover the same scope, materials, and work areas, rather than comparing totals alone.

Terms you may hear include:
- Roof deck: the base beneath the roof covering.
- Underlayment: a protective layer installed beneath the roof covering.
- Flashing: material used around roof joints and openings to help direct water away.
