Scope, timing, and details to compare

A metal roofing project discussion should cover the existing roof, the proposed material and system, preparation, installation tasks, and cleanup. Ask how the proposal addresses roof edges, joints, penetrations, and any areas that may need repair. The contractor should explain what is included and how unexpected conditions would be brought to your attention.

Key facts to keep in view:
- What it covers: confirm which roof sections are included, what will be removed or retained, and what installation and cleanup tasks are specified.
- Typical timing: the schedule depends on the size and shape of the roof, the work involved, material availability, and conditions uncovered during the job. Ask for the expected start and duration for your project.
- Cost factors: material selection, roof layout, access, preparation, and repair needs can affect cost. Compare written proposals with similar scopes instead of comparing totals alone.
- Provider comparison: ask about experience with the system being proposed, material specifications, property protection, cleanup, communication, and how changes are handled.

You may hear a few roofing terms. Underlayment is a layer installed beneath the roof covering. Flashing helps seal transitions and joints. A panel is a section of metal roof covering. Ask how each item is addressed in the proposal, including what applies to your particular roof.
