What a repair visit may cover

The scope depends on the roof’s condition and the source of the problem. A contractor may need to inspect the affected area, identify what materials or components need attention, and explain the next steps. Timing depends on the size and nature of the work, access to the roof, and conditions at the time. Cost generally depends on the repair scope, materials, and any related work needed; ask for a written description before approving a project.

Key facts to discuss:
- What part of the roof appears affected, and what signs were observed.
- What the contractor found and which repairs are being proposed.
- How long the work is expected to take, and what could affect the schedule.
- Which materials and related tasks are included in the estimate.

When comparing providers, look at how clearly each one explains the cause, repair plan, materials, timing, and exclusions. Ask whether the recommendation addresses the source of the concern, not only a visible symptom. A few terms you may hear: “flashing” is material used to direct water around roof joints and openings; “underlayment” is a layer installed beneath roofing material; and “scope” means the tasks included in the proposed work.
